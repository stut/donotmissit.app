Notifications are easy to miss.
This isn't.

DoNotMissIt sits in your Mac's menu bar. Shortly before each meeting, it covers every display with a card showing the meeting and a live countdown. Press Return to join the call, or Esc to dismiss.

For macOS Tahoe 26 or later.

From the menu bar to the meeting

It stays out of the way until it matters, then makes sure you can't look past it.

  1. 8m

    In the menu bar

    Your next meeting and the time until it starts. Click it to see what's coming up, and choose one to preview its alert.

  2. 0:42

    On every screen

    Shortly before the start, a card covers every display. It finds the join link in the event's URL, location or notes, so Return takes you straight into the call.

  3. +0:27

    Running late

    Once the start time has passed, the ring turns red and counts up, so you know exactly how late you are.

Try it: open Daily Standup · 8m in the menu bar at the top of this page and pick a meeting.

The details

It reads the calendars already on your Mac, so iCloud, Google, Exchange and any other account you've added in System Settings all work.

Meetings

Join links
Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, Whereby and GoTo.
In person
No link means the card shows the location and just a Dismiss button.
Same start time
Events starting together are combined into one alert.
Two calendars
A meeting that appears in both only alerts once.
Skipped
All-day, cancelled and declined events.
After sleep
If a meeting started in the last 5 minutes while your Mac was asleep, it alerts when you wake it.

Settings

Remind me
At the start time, or 1 to 10 minutes before.
Look ahead
How far ahead the menu bar looks for meetings, from 4 hours to a month.
Start at login
Your choice.

Your calendar never leaves your Mac.

Private

The app has no analytics, no advertising and no accounts. It doesn't collect, store or transmit any personal data, and it runs in Apple's App Sandbox with no permission to use the network.
